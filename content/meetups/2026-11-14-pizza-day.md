We organized a pizza day — boxes open, phones face-down, the usual Saturday crowd filling out into a longer afternoon.

Thanks [Troy](https://www.linkedin.com/in/troy-sadkowsky/) for making it happen. We had a good chat about connecting: how people find each other in a small tech scene, what keeps someone coming back after the first visit, and how a shared meal does work that a slide deck never will.

No talks on the calendar this time. Just builders in one room, catching up on what everyone is shipping, and leaving with a few more names worth texting next week.
